Why Pseudo-Random Numbers (`Math.random()`) Are Dangerous for Passwords

Standard JavaScript Math.random() is not cryptographically secure and can be predicted by malicious actors. SHAHRIYAR TOOLS Password Generator strictly utilizes window.crypto.getRandomValues(), leveraging hardware entropy from your device's operating system.

Password Entropy & Brute-Force Resilience

Entropy measures the unpredictability of a password in bits. A 20-character password composed of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols delivers over 120 bits of entropy, requiring billions of years for even supercomputing clusters to brute force.

Memory Only

Generated passwords exist solely in volatile computer RAM while this tab is open. They are never written to disk, cookies, or remote databases.
