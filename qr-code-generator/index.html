High-Precision QR Codes for Print & Digital Media

Quick Response (QR) codes connect the physical world to online experiences. Whether you are printing restaurant menus, Wi-Fi login table tents, business cards, or packaging, having high error correction and crisp vector export is essential for instant smartphone scanning.

Best Practices for Printable QR Codes

Always ensure strong contrast between the foreground and background colors. Dark QR codes on clean white backgrounds scan most reliably across all camera lighting conditions. When embedding a center logo, keep error correction set to High (H) so scanners can read the code even if the logo covers up to 30% of the center data area.
