Designing Accessible, Harmonious Color Palettes

Effective color choice defines brand identity and ensures high user engagement. However, digital design requires more than aesthetic appeal: accessibility guidelines (WCAG 2.2 AA) dictate that regular text must maintain a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 against its background.

Color Harmony Principles

Analogous schemes place adjacent hues on the 360-degree color wheel, creating soothing natural tones. Complementary schemes place opposing colors for high contrast call-to-action buttons, while triadic palettes introduce dynamic balance.
