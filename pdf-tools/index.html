Private, Client-Side PDF Tools for Sensitive Documents

PDF documents frequently contain private financial statements, legal contracts, identification papers, and medical records. Uploading these files to unknown cloud conversion portals exposes your personal data to risk.

SHAHRIYAR TOOLS PDF Toolkit utilizes WebAssembly and Web Worker technology to inspect, parse, merge, and split PDF structures entirely inside your local browser instance. No documents are transmitted across the internet, ensuring strict compliance with personal and corporate privacy standards.
