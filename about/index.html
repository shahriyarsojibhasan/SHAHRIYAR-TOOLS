About SHAHRIYAR TOOLS

An independent, privacy-conscious suite of online utilities engineered for creators, developers, students, and businesses.

Platform Philosophy

The internet is inundated with utility websites overloaded with intrusive tracking scripts, paywalls, and mandatory account sign-ups. Many tools upload sensitive private files—such as financial ledgers, photos, and legal contracts—to remote cloud servers without clear data governance.

SHAHRIYAR TOOLS was established with a singular architectural mandate: Compute locally whenever technically possible. By harnessing modern browser standards (HTML5 Canvas, Web Crypto API, Web Workers, and WebAssembly), our tools execute complex media processing, cryptography, and calculations directly in your device's memory.

Core Principles

  • Privacy by Default: Zero file uploads. Your images, PDFs, and text stay on your device.
  • Zero Cost: 100% free with no paywalls or locked features.
  • Speed & Responsiveness: Instant execution without queue times or server latency.
  • Bespoke Interface Design: Every tool features a dedicated user experience tailored to its specific function rather than a generic template.
