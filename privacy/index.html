Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Overview & Architectural Pledge

At SHAHRIYAR TOOLS (accessible at tools.shahriyarsojibhasan.top), user privacy is not an afterthought—it is the foundational design constraint of the entire platform. Unlike traditional online conversion services, our utilities operate almost exclusively within your local web browser.

2. Data Processing & Storage

When you use our utilities (such as Image Compressor, Batch Image Converter, PDF Toolkit, QR Code Generator, Password Generator, Word Counter, or Invoice Generator):

  • No File Uploads: Your documents, photos, text, and financial numbers are never uploaded to our servers, third-party cloud buckets, or remote APIs.
  • Volatile Memory Only: Files are read through native browser APIs (such as the HTML5 File API and Canvas API) into volatile local RAM and discarded as soon as you close or refresh the page.
  • Zero Data Retention: We do not log, inspect, store, or sell your files or personal information.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

SHAHRIYAR TOOLS does not use invasive tracking cookies or commercial ad beacons. Certain tools (such as the Invoice Generator draft feature) may optionally utilize your browser’s standard localStorage to allow you to resume your work upon returning. This data never leaves your computer.

4. Third-Party Libraries

Our client-side scripts load standard open-source utility engines (such as pdf-lib, qrcode.js, and marked.js) via reputable, publicly audited Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) including cdnjs and jsDelivr.

5. Contact Information

If you have any inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy or the platform's security architecture, you may reach the platform owner at shahriyarsojibhasan.top.
