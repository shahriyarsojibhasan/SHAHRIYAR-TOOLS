Why Use Browser-Native Image Compression?

Large image files significantly slow down page load times, waste mobile bandwidth, and harm your Google Core Web Vitals rankings (specifically Largest Contentful Paint). Traditional online image compressors require you to upload private family photos, confidential business documents, or proprietary creative assets to remote servers.

SHAHRIYAR TOOLS Image Compressor processes your photos directly inside your browser using HTML5 Canvas rendering and the modern Web Offscreen API. Your images never touch an external server, giving you zero upload delay, unlimited file compression, and total security.

How to Compress Images in 3 Simple Steps

  1. Upload Your Image

    Drag and drop your JPG, PNG, or WebP photo into the upload box or click to select from your file browser.

  2. Adjust Quality & Dimensions

    Move the compression quality slider or choose a quick preset (50%, 75%, 90%). Check the live before/after slider to ensure visual clarity.

  3. Download Instantly

    Click the green "Download Compressed Image" button to save the optimized file directly to your downloads folder.
